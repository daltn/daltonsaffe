I started using the internet in the early 2000s through 56k dial-up, napster, myspace, & music blogs. We now live in a time of fiber-optic broadband and we funnel our interactions online through social media platforms that profit off our attention and want to keep us on the endless scroll because it pads their bottom line.

Endless scroll out, blogs in. Welcome to my slice of the internet where I'll be sharing my writing, art, & music through blog posts and an email list so you don't have to follow me anywhere.

This site is built using 11ty & Claude Code, the images are stored in a Cloudflare R2 bucket, and the email list is sent through EmailOctopus.
